## What to build

Add six warn-tier tells to the `aggressive` preset: `tutorial-signposting`, `contrast-adverb`, `strawman-tail`, `restatement-marker`, `numbered-lead-in` and `claude-figurative`. After this, a writer who extends `aggressive` gets a smell on tutorial signposts such as `Let's dive in.`, on contrast adverbs such as `not simply`, on straw-man tails such as `not the other way around`, on restatements such as `In other words`, on numbered lead-ins such as `Three key reasons:`, and on figurative words such as `load-bearing`.

`strawman-tail` takes only the low-noise tokens: the bare `rather than` and `instead of` tails stay silent. `claude-figurative` leaves out `seams` and `quietly`. `contrast-adverb` keeps the `less like ... and more like` form. Start from the draft regexes in the survey.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The six tells exist in `aggressive` at tier `warn`, each with a message that says what to do instead.
- [ ] Every example phrase the survey lists for these tells fires the tell, and every false-positive note in the survey is a negative case that stays silent, in the survey-tell case tables of the rules tests.
- [ ] Each tell has a case in the golden fixture that covers every tell. The three golden files are regenerated, and the PR says the golden change is intended.
- [ ] No new tell shares a name and phase with a tell in `recommended`, so extending both presets only adds tells.
- [ ] The full test suite and `deslopper lint` on the repo pass.
