## What to build

Add four warn-tier tells to the `aggressive` preset: `missing-piece`, `contrarian-hook`, `overreach` and `invented-concept-label`. After this, a writer who extends `aggressive` gets a smell on claims of an overlooked gap such as `the missing piece` or `the blind spot everyone missed`, on contrarian hooks such as `Most teams get this backwards.`, on overreach such as `there's no denying`, and on invented labels such as `the context tax`.

`contrarian-hook` fires only on the full two-sentence form on one line and on the `get this backwards` form. `most people think` alone stays silent. Start from the draft regexes in the survey.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four tells exist in `aggressive` at tier `warn`, each with a message that says what to do instead.
- [ ] Every example phrase the survey lists for these tells fires the tell, and every false-positive note in the survey is a negative case that stays silent, in the survey-tell case tables of the rules tests.
- [ ] Each tell has a case in the golden fixture that covers every tell. The three golden files are regenerated, and the PR says the golden change is intended.
- [ ] No new tell shares a name and phase with a tell in `recommended`, so extending both presets only adds tells.
- [ ] The full test suite and `deslopper lint` on the repo pass.
