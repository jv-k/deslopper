## What to build

Add four warn-tier tells to the `recommended` preset: `signposting`, `urgency-inflation`, `stacked-hedge` and `tradeoff-dodge`. After this, a writer who extends `recommended` gets a smell on essay signposts such as `In conclusion,` and `Key takeaways`, on urgency inflation such as `cannot be overstated`, on stacked hedges such as `could potentially fail`, and on `without sacrificing` or `without compromising`.

`signposting` takes only the essay closers and openers named in #67. The tutorial phrases belong to ticket 4. Start from the draft regexes in the survey, `docs/research/slop-tells-survey.md`, which #66 adds. Read #66 from its branch if it has not merged yet.
