## What to build

Add four warn-tier tells to the `recommended` preset: `pithy-fragment`, `narrative-pivot`, `negative-listing` and `business-jargon`. After this, a writer who extends `recommended` gets a smell on slogans such as `Dense, not cramped.`, on staged turns such as `Then something shifted.`, on negative listings such as `Not a bug. Not a feature. A design flaw.`, and on jargon such as `move the needle`.

`pithy-fragment` has no `i` flag, because its capital letter anchors the start of a sentence. `business-jargon` uses `words`. Start from the draft regexes in the survey.
